Potato

Get your team to say the phrase on screen without using any of its words.

  1. Describe the phrase. Your team guesses out loud.
  2. When they get it, tap the button and pass the potato to the other team.
  3. The beeps get faster. At the buzzer, the team holding the potato loses the round and the other team scores.

One phone: pass it back and forth. Online: everyone uses their own device, in the same room or on a call.

Phrase Potato

  1. Describe the phrase. Your team guesses out loud.
  2. When they get it, pass the potato to the other team.
  3. At the buzzer, the team holding the potato loses the round and the other team scores.

Tip: Play like Homo erectus! Only use one-syllable words to describe your phrase!
